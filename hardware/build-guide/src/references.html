Datasheets

Manufacturer datasheets for the purchased parts. Documents open on the publisher’s site or an identified supplier’s site and require internet access. Where no datasheet exists, the table links the manufacturer’s product page. Some documents cover a whole product family; for those, check that you’re reading the variant listed in the parts list.

Sources and link-check dates (JSON). Generic items are chosen by the specifications in the parts list.
