Open the project. Slice one plate at a time. The 3MF files contain the parts, their settings and the material profiles. They contain no ready-to-print jobs. Before you send a plate, check its slice in the Bambu Studio preview for your printer and filament.

Material numbers are not AMS slots

Each plate has its own material profile. The number in a profile name is not an AMS slot. For example, “05 Blue PLA – B1” is the name of the blue arm plate’s profile. It does not mean slot 5 of the AMS (Bambu’s automatic material system). When you send a plate, map its profile to any slot that holds a suitable spool. Plates 1 and 3 (profiles W1 and W2) can share a white spool. They keep their own bed and cooling settings.

Keep these profiles. Leave “Synchronize filament list from AMS” off, because it replaces them. All ten plates in the main project are PLA, including the copper silk PLA plates. You can replace plate 10 (the antenna ball) with the optional clear PETG project.

Before printing

  1. Select the plate. Keep the part orientations, per-part settings, support blockers and brims. The base prints roof-down. The front microphone grille prints flat, front-down. Keep all of the base’s supplied support blockers. Some of them protect the front nut-entry channels and the open rear and power jack nut slots. The extra material around the bottom of each part is a brim for bed adhesion. It is not part of the design.
  2. Check that your filament, printer, nozzle and build plate match. Re-slice the plate. Inspect the first layer, the supports and any holes that must fit hardware. If you change the printer or material, check the slice again. Keep the printer’s own start and end code.
  3. Clean the build plate. Turn bed leveling on. Turn timelapse off. For plate 2 (black, K1), turn flow dynamics calibration off. The printer’s calibration line runs through that plate’s front brim. If you need calibration, run it separately. The other plates leave that strip clear. If you rearrange a plate, check the strip again.
  4. For PLA, open the printer door if your printer and filament call for it. Keep drafts off the bed. The profiles keep Bambu’s bed-temperature alert.

After printing, clean and check each part as in Clean and check each print. That step lists every cleanup task in one place.

The ten plates

Plate numbers name the plates in the project. They are not the print order. Print the plates in the order the build first needs them, so you can start building sooner. The order is 2 and 7, then 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 5, 10 and 9. Plates 1 and 3 hold the white parts. The head shell prints on its own. The belt and head straps share one gray plate. Optional clear PETG ball

This project has one antenna ball (A05) and replaces PLA plate 10. Print one or the other. It keeps the same thread blocker and settings. It starts from Bambu’s Generic PETG profile. Its settings: 255 °C nozzle, 70 °C Textured PEI bed, 0.95 flow ratio, 12 mm³/s limit and 40–90% part fan.

Estimated 22 min 26 s, 1.36 g. Before you use a generic profile, check your spool’s label. Check the thread fit and clarity on your first print.
