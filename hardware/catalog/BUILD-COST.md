# Build cost

**About $212 USD in parts and materials for one robot.** Prices checked October 1, 2026, before tax, shipping and any import charges. This counts only the filament, fasteners and consumables used, not the leftovers in full packs. Tools, optional parts, paint and running/API costs are excluded. Prices and material choices vary.

The tables below are a dated estimate, not a checkout quote. Pack prices help with shopping; buy only what you do not already have. Wire, glue, tape and other cut-material amounts are planning allowances, not measured assembly consumption. Generic product links are examples; use the specifications in the parts list.

## Filament

The ten selected [print plates](../printing/current/manifest.json) estimate **685.50 g**. Their slicer estimates include modeled print extrusion, supports and brims; separate machine calibration/purge and failed prints are not budgeted. ELEGOO standard PLA is priced at $13.99/kg and silk copper at $18.99/kg. No charge is added for buying multiple full spools. These are cost assumptions; use a print profile suited to your filament.
